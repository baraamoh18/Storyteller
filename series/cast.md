# Cast list: The Beginning After the End

One entry per character. Copy the empty template at the bottom for each new one.
Short notes are fine: a few words per line is enough. Leave a line out if you don't know it.

What each line is for:

- **Name / also called**: how bubbles and narration refer to them ("Professor Geist", "the brat"), so I can tell who is talking.
- **Look**: what tells them apart in a panel: hair, eyes, clothes, weapon, scars. This is the most important line.
- **Magic / colour**: their element and its colour on the page. It decides the colour of the particles and flashes in their scenes.
- **Role**: who they are to Arthur (friend, rival, teacher, enemy). It helps me pick the camera moves and music for a scene.
- **Personality / how they talk**: calm, smug, loud, shy. Used for the bubble timing now, and for the voices later.
- **First seen**: the chapter they first appear in, so I know when to expect them.

## Arthur Leywin

- **Also called**: Art; King Grey (past life); Note (masked adventurer alias); Papa (by Sylvie); Brat; Disciplinary Committee Officer; Professor Leywin; Lance Godspell
- **Look**: long red-auburn hair (often tied back at Xyrus), bright azure-blue eyes, slim build; wears black Xyrus Disciplinary Committee military uniform with a gold shoulder cord (and a white mask as "Note"); carries *Dawn's Ballad* (translucent cyan-turquoise sword, often wrapped in cloth). As King Grey: short pale-blonde hair, heavy muscular build, yellow tunic or full plate armour. In Realmheart: pale blonde/white hair, glowing lavender eyes, violet-gold runic markings across skin
- **Magic / colour**: in the Professor Geist duel, pure blue mana strengthening his body and sword (plus a compressed green-white wind blade at the end); overall Quadra-Elemental Augmenter — red-orange/blue fire, blue water/ice, green/white wind, amber-brown earth, crackling yellow-white/blue lightning, and glowing violet-purple Aether
- **Role**: the main character; a reincarnated king, now a first-year student and Disciplinary Committee officer at Xyrus Academy
- **Personality / how they talk**: calm, controlled, dry humour; teases opponents quietly ("Going easy, huh?"), drops to freezing cold and ruthless when his family or friends are threatened
- **First seen**: Webcomic Episode 1 / Novel Chapter 1

## Professor Geist

- **Also called**: the professor; Practical Mana Manipulation teacher
- **Look**: spiky brown hair, white headband, glasses, scar on the cheek; armoured shoulder and bracers, dark cloak with an emblem, broadsword
- **Magic / colour**: Fire Augmenter — blue flame wrapped around his sword ("BURN"); red-orange aura when he powers up
