# Cast list: The Beginning After the End

One entry per character. Copy the empty template at the bottom for each new one.
Short notes are fine: a few words per line is enough. Leave a line out if you don't know it.

What each line is for:

- **Name / also called**: how bubbles and narration refer to them ("Professor Geist", "the brat"), so I can tell who is talking.
- **Look**: what tells them apart in a panel: hair, eyes, clothes, weapon, scars. This is the most important line.
- **Magic / colour**: their element and its colour on the page. It decides the colour of the particles and flashes in their scenes.
- **Role**: who they are to Arthur (friend, rival, teacher, enemy). It helps me pick the camera moves and music for a scene.
- **Personality / how they talk**: calm, smug, loud, shy. Used for the bubble timing now, and for the voices later.
- **First seen**: the chapter they first appear in, so I know when to expect them.

I filled in the two characters from the duel chapter as examples. Please correct anything I got wrong.
